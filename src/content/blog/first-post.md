Hello, let me introduce myself. My online name will be Konan. I have decided to open this blog to learn about computer science. I am currently a systems engineering student, and I think it’s a good idea to open this blog as a personal project. I would like to upload my local projects or even enterprise-level projects, although I would prefer to do it all anonymously for the community. I hope to receive ideas, suggestions, and help from the community, and above all, to learn from the people and the community that exists on the internet.

Basically, this project will focus on building projects and learning, allowing people to share their opinions and ideas, while also keeping a record of the projects. Additionally, I want to document real work experiences so that people can give feedback and learn from them. I also want to keep a record over time of the projects I have worked on and my progress as a professional and as a person in this vast field.

Finally, I want this first post to be somewhat informative for those who want to check out the projects or posts I will be making on any topic that may come up over time. Best regards to all readers.
